Backend requires copying `backend/.env.example` to `backend/.env` and supplying `JWT_SECRET`, `GEMINI_API_KEY`, `DATABASE_URL`, and optionally `CORS_ORIGIN` / `UPLOAD_DIR`; startup aborts if any of the three required env vars are missing. Policy templates are seeded automatically at process start via `seedPolicyTemplates()`. Frontend uses Vite (`vite.config.ts`) and is configured for deployment on Vercel (`vercel.json`); backend is configured for Railway (`railway.toml`).
