Flash Claim

Smart Vehicle Insurance Claim System

Faster, fairer, fraud-resistant motor insurance claims for Sri Lanka.

❗ Problem

Motor insurance claims in Sri Lanka are slow, opaque, and easy to exploit. Policyholders wait days for assessors, garages inflate repair bills, and insurers struggle to verify damage and detect fraud without expensive manual review.

Daysto settle a typical claim
Highvariance in garage quotes
Manualdocument & damage verification
Paper-heavycommunication between parties

💡 Solution

Flash Claim is a three-role platform — policyholder, garage, and admin — that digitises the entire claim lifecycle from incident report to payout confirmation.

  • Policyholders upload accident details, photos, and documents; track status in real time.
  • Garages receive assigned claims, submit itemised estimates, and update repair progress.
  • Admins / insurers review AI-assisted damage assessments, reconcile garage quotes, set final claimable values, and message policyholders.

Everything is connected: one claim record flows through AI damage analysis, repair estimation, garage review, payout calculation, and final settlement.

🤖 AI Usage

Gemini powers several decision-support features behind the scenes. Outputs are deterministic numbers where possible, with narrative explanations generated only when needed.

Damage detection from photos Structured repair estimates Garage vs AI quote reconciliation Fraud scoring Incident ↔ damage consistency
  • Damage analysis — identifies damage type, severity, location, and affected parts from uploaded images.
  • Repair estimation — computes Sri Lankan-market parts and labour costs with vehicle-class scaling.
  • Reconciliation — compares garage line items against the AI estimate, flagging overcharges, missed damage, and labour discrepancies.
  • Fraud scoring — blends rule-based signals (policy recency, duplicate plates, document issues) with an LLM consistency check.

📈 Impact

Flash Claim reduces settlement time and human bias by automating the heavy-lifting steps while keeping the insurer in control of final decisions.
